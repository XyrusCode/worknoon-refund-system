# WORKNOON Refund Policy

## Eligibility

1. **Standard orders** are eligible for full refund within **30 days** of delivery date.
2. **Final sale items** are **not eligible** for any refund or exchange.
3. Orders older than **30 days** from delivery are **not eligible** for refund.

## Amount Thresholds

4. Refunds **above $500** require **human review** (escalated to support manager).
5. Refunds **at or below $500** may be auto-approved by the AI system if policy conditions are met.

## Condition-Based Rules

6. **Damaged items** may qualify for approval if the customer provides evidence of damage.
7. **Incorrect items** (wrong product shipped) qualify for full refund.
8. Items returned in **perfect condition** within 30 days qualify for full refund.

## Escalation Triggers

9. **Suspicious requests**: conflicting information, repeated refund attempts, or mismatched details must be escalated.
10. **Partial refund requests** over $200 require human review.
11. Any request that **cannot be verified** against order data must be escalated.

## Denial Conditions

12. Requests for items explicitly marked as **final sale** are denied.
13. Requests **outside the 30-day window** are denied unless escalation is warranted.
14. **Fraudulent or policy-violating** requests are denied with documentation.

## Documentation Requirements

15. All refund decisions must include:
    - Decision (Approved / Denied / Escalated)
    - Reasoning based on policy rules
    - Any policy violations identified
    - Confidence score (0-1)
